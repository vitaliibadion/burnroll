What BurnRoll does

BurnRoll is an on-device iPhone camera roll cleaner. You review photos, add items to a burn queue, and confirm before Apple Photos moves them to Recently Deleted. Photos are not uploaded to BurnRoll servers.

BurnRoll Pro

BurnRoll Pro is optional. It is sold as an auto-renewable subscription through Apple: Weekly (3-day free trial, then billed weekly), Monthly, and Yearly. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Prices shown in the app are Apple’s local prices for your storefront.

To cancel, open iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → BurnRoll Pro, and cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. To restore a trial or plan on a new device, open BurnRoll → Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Terms of Use: Apple’s standard EULA. Privacy: BurnRoll Privacy Policy.

Photos permission

The app needs Photos access to show your library and, only after you confirm, move selected items to Recently Deleted. You can change access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → BurnRoll.

Reminders

Cleanup reminders are optional. If a reminder does not appear after taking new photos, open BurnRoll so it can count new items. iOS does not let BurnRoll watch Photos in the background.
